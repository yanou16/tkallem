# What next

## Submission version (under 100 words)

Over the next three months we will put Tkallem in front of 30 students preparing visa and Campus France interviews,
through student clubs and language centers, and check whether their mistakes per 100 words and their time before speaking
drop over five sessions. We will add the scenarios they ask for, such as scholarship interviews, and a French interface.
It stays free to try with daily answers; intensive preparation packs and language-center partnerships would pay for it.
A spoken answer costs us less than half a cent today.

## The longer plan

1. **Real students first (weeks 1 to 4).** Put the link in the hands of 30 students and read their sessions with them.
   Measure with the numbers the app already keeps: time before speaking, mistakes per 100 words, confidence before and after
   (`docs/IMPACT.md`). Fix what they hit before adding anything.
2. **Situations they actually meet.** Everyday scenes (a café, a taxi, the airport, a bank), then scholarship and job interviews.
   A scenario is one JSON file plus its starters and evaluation sentences.
3. **A French interface and Darija support**, since the students who freeze most are the ones who read English least comfortably.
4. **A path, not a list**: a first session that asks three questions (goal, level, time per day) and orders the scenarios for the student.
5. **Groups and teachers**: a class or a club sees progress numbers without seeing anyone's answers.
6. **Sustainability**: free daily answers stay; preparation packs for visa and Campus France seasons; partnerships with language centers.
